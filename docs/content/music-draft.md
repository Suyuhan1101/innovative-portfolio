## 开场

**On the keys, since I was four.**

I started learning piano at four. I enjoy playing a mix of pieces, from Flower Dance and Luv Letter to Mariage d'Amour. I also play guitar, drums and marimba at a beginner level.

## 演奏故事

**Eight bars of Waterloo**

Waterloo has a spirited, determined energy that I love. When I first learned it, my playing was halting. I remember spending several hours practising just eight bars.

## 听歌

**What I listen to**

I listen to piano, instrumental, pop and lyrical music. I like the resonance of harmonies and the energy a rhythm can bring. Music often keeps me company while I write or spend time on my own.

Flower Dance by DJ Okawari is a favourite. I love its atmosphere, and I also play it on piano just for the joy of it.
