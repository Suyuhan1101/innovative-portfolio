## 一点关于编程的故事

My interest in coding started with LEGO EV3. Every week, I made something small: a spinning top launcher, Tetris, a robot that played pool. My favorite part was the moment the machine actually worked.

Over time, I started building things for my own needs, including a small program for saving passwords. I once considered studying medicine, which led me to a project connecting healthcare and programming. These days, I'm more drawn to coding.

## 屏幕之外

Music keeps me company when I'm writing or spending time on my own. I enjoy lossless audio and have more than a hundred tracks in my collection. My favorite is Flower Dance by DJ Okawari. I love its atmosphere, and I play it on piano just for the joy of it.

I play piano, and I'm a beginner on guitar, drums and marimba. I also love high places, good food and trips with something unexpected to see — like watching alligators from a small boat in a swamp.
